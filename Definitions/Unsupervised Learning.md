## Plain English Definition
Unsupervised learning works with inputs but no observed response variable. The goal is to uncover patterns such as clusters, latent structure, or lower-dimensional summaries.

## Formal Definition
$$
\{x_1, x_2, \dots, x_n\}
$$
| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| $x_i$ | Input features for observation $i$ |
| $n$ | Number of observations |

## Intuitive Analogy
Unsupervised learning is like opening a box of mixed documents with no labels attached. You do not know the categories in advance, so you sort them by similarity and try to infer the structure from the contents themselves.

## When to Use It
- ✅ When you need to discover structure in data and no target variable exists.

## When It Breaks Down
- ❌ When you expect a single objectively correct grouping or when the apparent structure is mostly noise.
