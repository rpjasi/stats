## Plain English Definition
Regression is a supervised learning problem in which the response is continuous or quantitative. The aim is to predict a number, such as wage, sales, or temperature, from one or more inputs.

## Formal Definition
$$
Y \in \mathbb{R}
$$
| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| $Y$ | Quantitative response variable |
| $\mathbb{R}$ | The real numbers, indicating a numeric response |

## Intuitive Analogy
Regression is like estimating the price of a house from its size, location, and age. You are not choosing between labels; you are trying to place the outcome on a number line as accurately as possible.

## When to Use It
- ✅ When the target you want to predict is numeric and measured on a meaningful scale.

## When It Breaks Down
- ❌ When the response is categorical rather than numeric, or when the numeric scale is too noisy to support useful prediction.
